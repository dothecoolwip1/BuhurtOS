  • Start recovery text: “Clock action not confirmed. It may have reached the server. Check the clock, then retry this same action.” A fresh start succeeded after recovery. Cause not established; no corresponding console error.
  • QR console errors occurred at 14:22:24.680Z and 14:22:48.029Z. Generic “[BuhurtOS] Object” errors accompanied hold refusals at 14:34:53.296Z and 14:47:44.341Z. Logs in later snapshots are cumulative, not additional QR failures.
