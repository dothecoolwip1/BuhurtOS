## 1. Marshall Jobs

### 1.1. Knight Marshal

- The knight marshal is in charge at a tournament, both inside and outside the list.
- There may be more than one Knight Marshal for tournaments like Conference or World Cup.
- A trainee marshal cannot be a Knight Marshal unless it is necessary.

### 1.2. Field Marshal

- The marshal inside the list in Buhurt.
- A trainee marshal cannot be a field marshal unless it is necessary.

### 1.3. Lines Marshal

- The marshal outside the list in Buhurt.
- Acting as counters for Duels, and outrance,

## 2. Accreditation Reference Accreditation document

### 2.1. Trainee - Level 4

A Trainee Marshal is the first step toward achieving international accreditation as a marshal. For further details, please refer to the Marshal Accreditation document.

### 2.2. Regional - Level 3

A Regional Marshal is an individual who has gained experience working at multiple events, successfully passed the Trainee test, and is now qualified to marshal inside the list.

### 2.3. Conference - Level 2

A Conference Marshal is an individual with extensive knowledge of the rules and has worked at various events. They have completed their exam, Interview and are on track for International accreditation.

### 2.4. International - Level 1

An International Marshal is the final level of accreditation, achieved by individuals with extensive experience in high-level events and demonstrating exceptional skills and knowledge.

## 3. Marshal Titles

### 3.1. Buhurt International Head Marshal

- Organizer Marshal for the world.
- Will be selected from one of the Conference Head Marshals.

### 3.2. Conference Head Marshal

- Organizer Marshal for Conference.
- Will be selected from one of the Regional Head Marshals in that Conference.

### 3.3. Buhurt International Advisor

- Special nomination for Highly Experienced Marshals that do not hold another title.
- They advise the Buhurt International Head Marshal.
- This position will be selected by the Buhurt International Committee.

### 3.4. Deputy Conference Head Marshal

- Deputy Organizer Marshal for Conference.
- Will be selected from one of the Regional Head Marshals in that Conference.

### 3.5. Regional Head Marshal

- Organizer Marshal for Region. Example: UK Head Marshal. France Head Marshal.

### 3.6. Senior International Marshal

- International marshals that are a very high level but are not regional heads or committee members.
- The Buhurt International marshal committee will give this title to a marshal.

## 5. Marshal uniforms

This section displays all the rules and regulations about uniforms for marshals.

### 5.1. Knight marshal

- Knight Marshals wear 14th to 16th century period clothes that are yellow and black striped.

### 5.2. Field marshal and line marshal

- All accredited marshals who are not Knight Marshals at a tournament wear 14th to 16th century period clothes that are yellow.

### 5.3. Trainee marshal

- Trainee Marshals wear 14th to 16th century period clothes that are not yellow, with a yellow hood.

### 5.4. Modern events

- Marshals are allowed to wear a yellow shirt at modern events. Appropriate pants and shoes must be worn.
