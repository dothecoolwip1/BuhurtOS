Everything here is FICTIONAL. Every organization, team, event and fighter name ends in `-test`.
Disciplines: `5v5`, `3v3`, `LS` (Longsword), `SS` (Sword & Shield), `PA` (Polearm). "melee" means 5v5 and 3v3 (same gender division as the fighter). Female divisions are written "Female 5v5" etc.
Fields: `Name | Gender | Age | City | Province | Joined | Disciplines | Notes` (age as of Oct 2026; birth_year = 2026 - age).
`attend` notes are binding instructions for how often the fighter appears in the historical events and at the Rumble.

## Organization
Northern Armored Combat League-test, short NACL-test, kind: regional governing organization, Canada, region Western Canada.
Backstory: formed by several Western Canadian armored combat clubs wanting a shared competition calendar, consistent tournament standards, regional rankings, fighter records and easier travel between clubs. Alberta-heavy at first, gradually expanded relationships into British Columbia, Saskatchewan and Manitoba. Teams vary substantially in size, experience, travel frequency and competitive success.
